## Computer Science Honour Code

This Honour Code is a set of guidelines and standards for Computer Science class.

### Academic Integrity

I understand I am expected to follow the assignment guidelines in the Course Outline. This includes:

* Only working on assignments within their designated spaces (i.e. CodeHS or Google Docs). 
* Only using generative AI when it is allowed and to the extent to which it is allowed.
* Being honest with all the sources I used for each assignment.
* Not sharing or posting my assignment solutions.
* Not letting classmates take a picture of my assignment solutions.
* Not letting classmates copy my assignment solutions.
* Not asking classmates to share their assignments with me.
* Not asking someone to do my assignments for me.

I understand that I should be able to explain the code, ideas, and decisions in any work that I submit.

### Collaboration

I understand that on most assignments, it is appropriate to ask others for debugging help or to discuss problem-solving strategies and code structure, but I need to do the actual coding on my own. 

When I get help from others on an assignment, I will declare within the assignment who helped me and how they helped me.

### Digital Citizenship

* I promise to uphold the standards of academic honesty and digital citizenship:
*  I will be responsible for my own work.
*  I will respect the work of others.
*  I will take responsibility for my decisions and actions.

By submitting an assignment, I confirm that I have followed this honour code and that the work I am submitting represents my own learning.
